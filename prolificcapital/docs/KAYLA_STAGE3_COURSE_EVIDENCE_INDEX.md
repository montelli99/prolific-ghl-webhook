### E13: Deal structure teaching — cash, stack, SubTo (TIER 1)

**Source:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:1-158`
**Speaker:** Jaxon
**Contact Path:** All
**Deal Type:** Cash, Stack, SubTo

**Key Excerpts:**
- Cash: "ARV after repair value, they take into consideration what are their repair costs and how much money will they make."
- Underwriting: "Take ARV, multiply by 0.7, subtract repair cost, subtract wholesale fee."
- Stack: "50% down at closing, seller holds 50%. Minimum 50% equity (hard floor). Preferred 65%+ equity (seller profitability). Free and clear."
- 10% Down: "0 to 10 or 15% down. Down payment must cover equity. Free and clear."
- SubTo: "Take over existing debt. Low equity. Pain point required. Typically 72 months max (negotiable). Don't say subject to over phone."

**Finding:** Explicit formulas taught by Jaxon. Equity has two thresholds (50% floor, 65% preferred). SubTo requires pain point, not just low equity. 72 months is typical maximum, negotiable.
