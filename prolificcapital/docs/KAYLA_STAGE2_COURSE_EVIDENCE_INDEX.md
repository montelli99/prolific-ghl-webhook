## CROSS-TRANSCRIPT CONSISTENCY ANALYSIS

### Contact Made Entry (S2-ENTRY-001)

| Source | What Authorizes Entry? |
|---|---|
| E1 (Call 1 transcript) | Call → CCC → contact card → notes → (implied: then stage movement) |
| E2 (Call 2 transcript) | Call → CCC → contact card → (implied: then stage movement) |
| E3 (CRM transcript) | CRM import with notes → then offer generation |
| E7 (List kickoff) | Call → notes → end-of-day lead sheet → AI offer |
| E9 (Master playbook) | Call → CCC → evaluate → GCJ → spreadsheet → offer sent |
| E12 (Stage guide) | INT → call → collect → CCC → notes → move to Contact Made |
| E13 (TRACK_STUDENT) | INT Sent → Stage 2 (FALSE CONFLICT — developer simplification) |

**Resolution:** Contact Made entry requires: completed call + CCC/contact card sent + notes recorded. Operator confirms entry. The TRACK_STUDENT "INT Sent" rule is a developer simplification that does not reflect course evidence.

### CCC Stage Relationship (S2-CCC-001)

| Source | When is CCC Sent? |
|---|---|
| E1 (Call 1) | Immediately after call ends |
| E2 (Call 2) | Immediately after call ends |
| E9 (Master playbook) | Step 5: After Call (before evaluation) |
| E12 (Stage guide) | During Stage 1, before moving to Contact Made |

**Resolution:** CCC is a post-call action sent immediately after the call. Its completion is confirmed as part of the Contact Made entry gate. It is not exclusively Stage 1 or Stage 2 — it is a post-call requirement verified at the stage boundary.

### F50/F10 Applicability (S2-F50-001)

| Source | Context |
|---|---|
| E8 (Scripts reference) | Explicitly "FACEBOOK MARKETPLACE" section headings |
| E9 (Master playbook) | Step 6 evaluation options for turnkey/renovation |
| E13 (TRACK_STUDENT) | Stage 2 actions after CCC |

**Resolution:** F50/F10 are creative financing proposal shortcuts. The scripts reference labels them as Facebook Marketplace tools, but the master playbook and stage guide present them as evaluation options. They are available as Stage 2 evaluation options when the deal type supports them, but they are not universal Stage 2 actions. They are COURSE_PATH_SPECIFIC.

### GCJ Timing (S2-GCJ-001)

| Source | When is GCJ Used? |
|---|---|
| E5 (PPC process) | End of initial PPC call (Kayla group chat) |
| E9 (Master playbook) | Step 8, after evaluation, before end-of-day |
| E11 (Objection handling) | When lead is hot, seller engaged |
| E4 (Follow-up transcript) | Around offer time ("those offers are out") |

**Resolution:** GCJ has multiple path-specific triggers: (1) PPC path: end of initial call, (2) Hot lead: when seller is engaged/countering, (3) Standard path: after Stage 2 evaluation, before/at handoff. GCJ is not a single universal trigger.

### Stage 2 Exit (S2-EXIT-001)

| Source | What Moves to Offer Ready? |
|---|---|
| E9 (Master playbook) | After evaluation, comps, Seth email → then "Move to Offer Sent" |
| E12 (Stage guide) | After evaluation, F50/F10, Seth email → move to Offer Ready |
| E13 (TRACK_STUDENT) | CCC Sent → Stage 3 (FALSE CONFLICT) |

**Resolution:** The operator moves to Offer Ready after completing Stage 2 evaluation and submitting the information package to the closer team. The closer team prepares the offer. The transition is operator-confirmed. The TRACK_STUDENT "CCC Sent" rule is a developer simplification.
