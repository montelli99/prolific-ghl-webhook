## What We Already Know

- A real conversation must happen. A text alone is not enough.
- Calling the listing agent counts. Calling the seller directly counts.
- CCC and contact card go after every completed call.
- Notes must be recorded.
- The 48-hour follow-up belongs after an offer is sent, not during Contact Made.
- Montelli never negotiates. He relays everything to you.

## What We Need You to Answer

### 1. When does a lead officially enter Contact Made?

After the call is done, notes are recorded, and CCC is sent — is that when
Montelli moves it to Contact Made? Or is there a different trigger?

### 2. Which facts must be collected before Contact Made work is finished?

We know to ask about roof, HVAC, occupancy, rent, lease, utilities, and
feedback. Which of these must be answered before moving forward, and which can
be marked "unknown" or "not provided" if the contact doesn't know?

### 3. Is CCC part of Stage 1 or Stage 2?

CCC is sent right after the call. Should Montelli confirm it was sent before
entering Contact Made, or while working in Contact Made?

### 4. What exactly does Montelli evaluate during Contact Made?

After confirming the call facts, does he classify the property as turnkey or
renovation? Check rental comps? Estimate repairs? Something else?

### 5. Are F50 and F10 used for pipeline leads?

F50 ("half your price now") and F10 ("10% now, rest in 24 months") appear in
the Facebook Marketplace section of the scripts. Are they also used during
Contact Made for regular list leads, or only for Facebook prospecting?

### 6. Who receives the handoff when Contact Made work is done?

Does Montelli send the lead to Seth for an LOI? To you? To Jaxon? Does it
depend on whether the property is turnkey or needs renovation?

### 7. When is the GCJ group chat created?

Does Montelli create the group chat with Jaxon during Contact Made, when the
offer is ready, after the offer is sent, or only when the seller is actively
engaged?

### 8. What moves a lead from Contact Made to Offer Ready?

Does Montelli move it when he finishes his evaluation? When you or Seth accept
the lead? When the offer is actually prepared?

### 9. What happens when a lead cannot go to Offer Ready?

If the seller is not motivated, the property doesn't fit, or information is
missing — where does the lead go? Does it stay in Contact Made? Move to
Declined? Something else?

### 10. How do PPC and FSBO leads fit into Contact Made?

PPC leads have their own scripts and require photos. Do they enter the same
Contact Made stage, or do they follow a different process?
