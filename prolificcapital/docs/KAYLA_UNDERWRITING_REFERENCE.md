## CASH OFFER UNDERWRITING

### 70% Quick Underwriting Formula

**Purpose:** Quick screening tool to determine maximum cash offer.

**Formula:** ARV x 0.70 - Repairs - Wholesale Fee = Max Offer

**Scope:** Cash wholesale deals only. Not applicable to seller finance or SubTo.

**Limitations:** This is a quick screening formula, not a binding offer calculation. The closer team runs full underwriting. The operator uses this to evaluate whether a deal is worth pursuing.

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:48-56`
**Speaker:** Jaxon
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

**How Jaxon Teaches It:**
1. Find ARV (similar sold properties on Redfin/Zillow in same neighborhood)
2. Multiply ARV x 0.70
3. Subtract repair estimate (what an investor would pay)
4. Subtract wholesale fee (your profit margin)
5. Result is your maximum offer

**Worked Example (Jaxon):**
- ARV: $100,000
- x 0.70 = $70,000
- Repairs: $30,000
- = $40,000 (investor price)
- Wholesale fee: $10,000
- Max offer: $30,000

**Key Constraint:** "We have to get a property at a discount. Investors buy wholesale deals at a discount, which means we have to get a discounted property at a discount." (Jaxon, `03-STEP3-Pt1:26-28`)

## 50% STACK (SELLER FINANCE)

### Equity Requirements

**Hard Floor:** Minimum 50% equity. Below this, the seller loses money after the 50% down payment.

**Preferred Threshold:** 65%+ equity. At this level, the seller makes money on the deal.

**Why Two Thresholds:** Jaxon teaches both: "must have a minimum of 50% equity" and "we like the equity to be at least 65%." The 50% floor prevents seller loss. The 65% preference ensures seller profitability.

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:63-67`
**Speaker:** Jaxon
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

### Free and Clear Requirement

**Rule:** Property must have no mortgage. Free and clear.

**Why:** "For seller finance deals, really we're looking for free and clear properties. That means there is no mortgage on the property." (Jaxon, `03-STEP3-Pt1:72-73`)

**How to Check:** propwire.com — enter address, check equity.

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:69-73`
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

### Structure

- 50% down at closing
- Seller holds remaining 50% (seller carry)
- No monthly payments on the in-between
- Remaining balance paid in full on or before balloon date
- Deed in lieu protects seller (if buyer defaults, seller keeps down payment + gets property back)

## 10% DOWN (SELLER FINANCE)

### Equity Requirements

**Rule:** Down payment must cover seller's remaining equity. 0-15% down range.

**Free and Clear:** Required. No mortgage on property.

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:69-71`
**Speaker:** Jaxon
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

### Structure

- 10% (or 0-15%) down at closing
- Seller carries remaining balance
- Balance payoff: "Our goal is to ensure it gets paid off quickly, it simply just depends on the rental income" (Master Playbook)
- Typically used for older or renovation properties

## SUBJECT-TO (SUB2)

### Requirements

**Low Equity:** Property has low equity (seller owes close to what it's worth and can't sell traditionally).

**Pain Point:** Required. Jaxon explicitly teaches: "nobody's just going to sub two just because it's low equity. There's got to be a pain point." Pain points include:
- Missing payments
- About to go into foreclosure
- Cannot keep up with mortgage
- Would lose money selling traditionally (after realtor commission, closing costs, buyer fixes)

**Good Interest Rate:** "I don't want to take over an interest rate that's at six, seven percent... If they got a mortgage at two, three, four, maybe low fives, you can't go get a mortgage for that, so it would make sense." (Jaxon, `03-STEP3-Pt1:133-136`)

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:95-143`
**Speaker:** Jaxon
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

### Balloon Term

**Guidance:** Typically 72 months maximum. Negotiable.

**Evidence:** Jaxon teaches "typically 72 months or more." Kayla negotiates 60 months in a live call (`12-STEP3-Pt2:176-177`). Jaxon uses "on or before month 72" language in live deals (`10-STEP3-Pt2:19-20`).

**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT` (guidance, not hard requirement)

### Communication Rule

**Do NOT say "subject to" over the phone.** People get scared. Use qualifying questions instead:
- "Would it make sense if we took over the debt and put some money in your pocket?"
- "Would it make sense if we just took those payments up? We built your credit for you."

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:113-128`
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT`

## 1% RENT SCREENING GUIDANCE

### Purpose

Quick screening tool to evaluate whether a property's rental income supports the purchase price.

### Rule

**Guidance:** We like to see monthly rent approximately 1% of purchase price.

**Scope:** Turnkey/good condition properties. Screening only. Not mandatory.

**Limitations:**
- Kayla uses preference language: "we like to see" (`13-STEP3-Pt3:8-9`)
- Jaxon explicitly downgrades it for fix and flips: "the 1% rule is not really the golden rule anymore" (`03-STEP3-Pt1:24-25`)
- For commercial: "the net operating income should be 1% of the overall purchase price, and that's before debt service" (`05-STEP3-Pt1:50-51`)

**Transcript:** `airei-course-notes/13-STEP3-Pt3-How-to-Find-Rental-Comps.txt:8-9`, `airei-course-notes/03-STEP3-Pt1-Deal-Structure-Cash-Stack-SubTo.txt:21-25`, `airei-course-notes/05-STEP3-Pt1-Dominican-Republic-Giveaway-Incentive.txt:50`
**Classification:** `COURSE_EXPLICIT` (screening guidance, not mandatory)

### What to Do If Below 1%

"If it's not, we can take a closer look and see if we can do midterms or short terms. Midterms are going to be traveling nursing tenants or short terms are going to be Airbnb." (Kayla, `13-STEP3-Pt3:9-11`)

## UNDERWRITING DATA REQUIREMENTS

### Required for All Offers

- ARV (After Repair Value)
- Asking/purchase price
- Repair estimate
- Market rent

### Required for Seller Finance (50% Stack, 10% Down)

- Equity percentage
- Mortgage balance (must be zero — free and clear)

### Required for Subject-To

- Existing mortgage balance
- Interest rate
- Monthly payment
- Pain point (reason seller needs to exit)
