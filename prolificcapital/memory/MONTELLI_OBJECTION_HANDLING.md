## HOT LEAD DEFINITION

A lead is HOT when seller/agent:
- Confirms the offer is being presented
- Counters with any specific terms
- Says they need to sell
- Has other buyers circling
- Asks about down payment, timeline, closing costs

**On hot lead:**
1. GCJ text shortcut → creates group chat with Kayla + seller
2. Email Kayla: "Hot lead — [address] — [counter details]"
3. STOP — Kayla/Jaxon take it from here
4. Montelli stays warm with seller

---

## THE GROUP CHAT (GCJ)

GCJ text shortcut is sent AFTER Montelli has confirmed the deal is real and the seller is engaged. It introduces Jaxon to the seller directly. From that point, **Kayla/Jaxon run the negotiation** in the group chat or by calling the seller directly.

Montelli's job after GCJ: stay warm, follow up every 3-5 days, make sure seller doesn't go dark.
