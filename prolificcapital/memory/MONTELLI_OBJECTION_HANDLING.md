## HOW MONTELLI REFERS TO KAYLA AND THE TEAM

**To the seller/agent — never names them specifically:**
- "My business partner" — never says "Kayla" or "Jaxon" to a seller or agent
- "My partner" — same rule
- "We" — always "we" not "I" when presenting or discussing the offer
- "Our lender" — when explaining DSCR qualification
- "Our assistant" — when asked how Montelli knows about the property

**In group chat (GCJ text shortcut):**
- In the GCJ text: "my business partner Jaxon" — because the seller is already in the chat and it's natural to introduce Jaxon there
- The offer itself comes from `homewithkaylamauser@gmail.com` so sellers see Kayla's name in the email

**Exact script for introducing the team:**
> "This offer comes from our company — we acquire properties using a DSCR loan which is solely based on what the property makes as a rental. As long as the rent covers the mortgage, we're good to go at the price we're asking. I'm going to give my lender a quick call and see how I can get approved. Is there a good email I can send over details to?"

## OBJECTION HANDLING — MONTELLI NEVER NEGOTIATES

**The one rule from the course:**
> "Noted — what I'll do is relay this over to my business partner and I'll get back with you. I look forward to aligning finer details with you."

**Montelli NEVER:**
- Makes a counter-offer on the call
- Negotiates price or terms directly
- Tells the seller "I'll raise our offer"
- Answers questions about interest rates, down payment amounts, or timelines
- Signs anything or sends contracts

**Montelli ALWAYS:**
- Listens completely
- Says "Noted — I'll bring my business partner in on this"
- Relays the exact objection + context to Kayla via email immediately
- Calls back when Kayla has the answer

## OBJECTION SCRIPTS (from course transcripts)

### Objection: "The offer is too low"
**Pivot script:**
> "That's exactly why I'm calling you, [agent name]. With the property still being listed for sale, it looks like your seller hasn't received any sufficient offers from buyers who intend to live in the property. Our lender has confirmed this would not be able to be a rental for anyone due to interest rates. Now I totally understand they want to sell out right — feel free to revisit this right before the listing agreement expires."

**Then ask for more business:**
> "Is there any other listings you need help getting paid on?"

---

### Objection: "I want to sell outright / no seller financing"
**Pivot script:**
> "I understand your intent to sell outright. Would it make better sense if I overpaid for the property here just a little bit and just paid them monthly most of what it would rent out for on a short-term balloon? That way they don't have to deal with tenants and toilets — we'll be responsible for tenants, toilets, taxes, and insurance. And we'll just pay them out as if they were the landlord."

---

### Objection: "I counter with 10% interest on the seller carry-back"
*(This is a specific terms objection — Montelli doesn't navigate this alone)*
**Script:**
> "Noted — let me bring my business partner in on this. Hold tight."

Then immediately email Kayla with the counter details. Kayla handles the math. Montelli delivers Kayla's response.

---

### Objection: "Your offer is well below what she can accept"
**Script (from actual course call):**
> "We offered [full asking price] and we're bringing 50% of that down at closing on a short-term loan. We came from Home with Kayla Mouser."

*(Never apologize for the offer — present it with confidence and let Kayla handle the rest)*

---

### Objection: "I want to see/walk the property first"
**Script:**
> "Our assistant drove past a few days ago and referred it to us. The photos online look amazing — they don't even do the property justice. We'll set up a home inspection just like any real estate transaction."

---

### Objection: "I have another buyer interested"
**Leverage script:**
> "We have other deals in motion too — we want to close fast. What would it take to get this done today?"

---

### Objection: "The property needs too much work"
**Pivot to subject-to or seller financing:**
> "Would it make better sense if I overpaid a little bit and just paid them monthly most of what it would rent out for on a short-term balloon? We handle all the repairs — you don't have to lift a finger."

---

### Objection: "Life happened — I need to sell now"
**Hot lead trigger** → Send GCJ text shortcut immediately → Montelli tells Kayla:
> "Hot lead — [address] — seller needs out, ready to move fast. Counter terms: [whatever they said]. Create group chat with Jaxon."

---

### Objection: "I don't understand your structure"
**Explanation script:**
> "We buy using a DSCR loan — our lender only looks at what the property makes as a rental. As long as the rent covers the mortgage, we're good to go at the price we're asking. We're putting 50% down at closing and the remaining balance in a short-term balloon — no monthly payments to you during that time. The deed transfers just like any other real estate transaction."

## HOT LEAD TRIGGERS — When to Alert Kayla Immediately

A lead is HOT when the seller or agent:
1. Confirms the offer is being presented to the seller
2. Asks about down payment, timeline, closing costs
3. Mentions other buyers circling
4. Says "I need to sell now"
5. Counters with specific terms (any counter = hot lead)
6. Asks about the DSCR loan structure or interest rate

**On any hot lead:**
1. Send GCJ text shortcut immediately (creates group chat with Kayla + seller)
2. Text Kayla: "Hot lead — [address] — [counter details]"
3. Kayla generates a counter response
4. Montelli calls seller back with Kayla's counter

---

## THE ONLY EXCEPTION TO "NEVER NEGOTIATE"

**On the initial agent call (Stage 1):** Montelli can ask qualifying questions and pitch the offer structure confidently. He explains how the DSCR loan works and can say "we come at full asking price with 50% down." But once the conversation moves to actual negotiation (counter-offer, terms discussion), Montelli stops and defers to Kayla.
