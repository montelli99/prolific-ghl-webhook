1
Pre-Call
Copy address into GHL as Opportunity Name · Save contact in phone: First Name "[Agent]", Company "[Address]" · Send INT text · Wait 30 sec → DIAL
2
Opener (SMILE. SLOW.)
"Happy [DAY], I'm calling regarding [ADDRESS] — I'm interested in potentially purchasing this as a rental for my portfolio. Did I catch you at a good time?"
3
Qualify: Feedback
"Photos look great — I'm SHOCKED it hasn't sold. Have you received any feedback from other buyers who've walked it?"
4
Qualify: Condition
"Regarding the roof and HVAC — when were those last installed?"

[If reno: "How would you rate condition 1-10? What would it need to be a 10?"]
5
Qualify: Occupancy
"Is it currently occupied or vacant?"

OCCUPIED → "Owner or tenant? What's the rent? Lease type?"
VACANT → "Why wouldn't the seller just rent it out?"
6
Wrap-Up
"Are utilities still on?"

"I'm really interested. I'd purchase outright using a DSCR loan — as long as the rent covers the mortgage, I'm good at asking price. I'll give my lender a call."
7
Get Email + Close
"What's a good email to send details?" · VERIFY letter by letter · "It was great connecting. Looking forward to aligning details. Have a great rest of your day. God bless."
8
Post-Call
Send CCC immediately · Update GHL with all notes · If qualified → draft LOI request to Seth · If no answer → send voice memo
🛑 BOUNDARIES — What you NEVER do
🛑
NEVER say these words
"Just checking in" · "Just following up" · "Subject to" over the phone · "Is the offer okay?" · "Did you get a chance to look at it?"
🛑
NEVER do these things
Send agreements or contracts · Discuss terms with sellers · Underwrite deals · Close deals · Say "I don't know" — say "I'll relay to my business partner"
✅
ALWAYS do these things
INT before every call · CCC after every call · "Noted — I'll relay to my business partner" for all questions · Ask "any other properties?" · Smile. Speak SLOW.
💡
Golden rules
Your energy is your currency · We're providing a solution, not selling · If they say no, they're comfortable — "Would you be opposed..." · Every no = not yet · "We play Pokemon — assist people into retirement"

💬 OBJECTION HANDLING — What to say back
"Your offer is too low"
"I completely understand. Our LOI is not a final number — it's a starting point. What we're offering is certainty of close: no financing contingencies, no appraisal risk, close in 14-21 days cash."
"We already have an offer"
"That's great. What are the terms? Cash or financed? Timeline? If your seller is comparing a financed buyer to a cash buyer, it's not apples-to-apples."
"That's way too risky"
"Not risky at all. 50% down. Deed in lieu pre-signed at closing. If we default, you keep ALL the money AND the property. 15 days mispayment → deeded back. You'd almost get paid twice."
"We want cash only, no seller financing"
"That's exactly why I'm calling. Your seller hasn't received offers from owner-occupants. Our lender confirmed: with current rates, a buyer who lives there can't qualify for this property."
"I don't work with investors"
"Completely understand. We're not flippers — we buy and hold long-term. We keep properties occupied and maintained. We're the most low-risk buyer you could find."
"I need to talk to my wife/husband/family"
"Totally fair — it's a big decision. When do you think they'll have a chance to discuss? [ADDRESS] is approaching [DOM] days on market. If timing lines up, we'd love to move."
"Have you seen the property?"
"Our assistant drove past a few days ago and referred it. Photos look great — I'm sure they don't do it justice. We'll set up a home inspection within 24 hours like any real estate transaction."
"Who's the end buyer?"
"Montelli with Prolific Capital. We buy SFH and small multi-family in cash. Long-term rentals — not flippers. My partner Jaxon and I close in 14-21 days. No contingencies. We pay all closing costs."
